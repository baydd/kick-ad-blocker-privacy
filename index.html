Privacy Policy — Kick Ad Blocker

Last updated: August 10, 2026

This privacy policy describes how the Kick Ad Blocker Chrome extension ("the extension", "we") handles information. The extension's only purpose is to block in-stream (SSAI) video ads on the kick.com website.

1. Information We Do NOT Collect

The extension does not collect, store, or transmit any of the following:

  • Personal identification information (name, email address, phone number)
  • Account credentials or passwords
  • Payment or financial information
  • Health or biometric data
  • Location data or IP addresses
  • Browsing history
  • Keystrokes, mouse movements, or any other input data
  • Websites content of any kind

2. Information Stored Locally

The extension stores a single flag in your browser's local storage (Chrome storage.local API) indicating whether the ad-blocking mode is active. This flag is used only to show the correct status in the extension popup and never leaves your device.

3. Network Requests

The extension makes a single request to the official Kick API (kick.com) to obtain the ad-free playback URL of the channel you are currently watching. This request is made in the page's own context and contains no personal data. No other network requests are made by the extension.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, rent, or share any user data with third parties. Because the extension does not collect user data, there is nothing to share.

5. Third-Party Content

The extension popup contains a donation button that links to buymeacoffee.com. Clicking this button is an optional action taken by the user and is subject to Buy Me a Coffee's own privacy policy. The button image is loaded directly from the Buy Me a Coffee CDN.

6. Children's Privacy

The extension does not knowingly collect any information from children under the age of 13.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

8. Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please contact the developer through the Chrome Web Store listing page of the Kick Ad Blocker extension.
